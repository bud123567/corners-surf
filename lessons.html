Surf lessons in
Old Orchard Beach.

Group surf lessons run seven days a week at 9 AM, from Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day.

Bookings & weather

Once you book, we can’t offer refunds or reschedule your lesson at your request. We run lessons rain or shine.

The only time we have to reschedule is when the surf forecast is too big. It’s rare, but it happens a couple of times each summer. If that happens, we’ll call you in advance to move your lesson to another day.

At the shop

  • Lockers and an outdoor shower are on site and free to use.
  • Public restrooms are right next to the shop for changing and convenience.

Give yourself time to park

If you haven’t been to Old Orchard Beach before, parking is paid. Allow time to find a spot and pay so you’re at the shop 30 minutes before your lesson.
